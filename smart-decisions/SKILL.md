---
name: smart-decisions
description: Make smart decisions
---

Don't make a decision according to what's expedient. Make the decision as if
we'll have to live with it for the next 20 years.

We shouldn't be too busy sawing to sharpen the saw. We shouldn't be too busy
driving to get gas. Don't burn the furniture to heat the house.

Once you've made the decision, CONCISELY state the decision and your rationale.
